Pines Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Pines ("we", "us") is a social app for couples and singles in the lifestyle community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Pines is intended only for adults aged 18 and older.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), date of birth (used only to confirm you are 18+ and to display your age), username, display name, and account type.
  • Profile information you provide: photos and videos, bio, gender, orientation, body type, height, interests, preferences, and relationship details. Some of this may be considered sensitive information; you choose what to share.
  • Identity verification: if you complete verification, a selfie and a photo of a government-issued ID. These images are used solely to confirm you are a real adult and are not shown to other users.
  • Location: with your permission, your device's approximate location, used to show nearby people and events and to display distance on profiles. You can turn this off in your device settings and hide distance in the app's Privacy settings.
  • Content and activity: posts, comments, likes, messages, event RSVPs, connections, blocks, reports, and reactions.
  • Device information: push notification token, device type, app version, and crash or error diagnostics.

How we use information

  • To operate the service: creating your profile, matching you with other members, delivering messages, and hosting events.
  • To keep the community safe: age gating, identity verification, reviewing reports, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To send push notifications you have opted into (messages, connections, likes, comments, RSVPs, and event reminders). You can change these in Settings.
  • To improve and secure the app and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who can see your information

Your profile, photos, posts, and comments are visible to other verified members according to your Privacy settings. Private album photos are only shared with members you approve. Direct and group messages are visible only to their participants. Verification images, email, exact location, and date of birth are never shown to other members.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run Pines, each bound to process data only on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), Expo (push notification delivery), Apple (App Store and notifications), and GIPHY (GIF search in chat; your search terms are sent to GIPHY).

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, posts, messages, and verification images from our systems within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records to comply with law or to enforce our Terms (for example, records of accounts removed for safety violations).

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or remove profile information at any time in the app.
  • Control location, camera, photo, and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Block or report any member from their profile or content.
  • Request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing us at the address below. Residents of certain regions (including the EU, UK, and California) have additional rights of access, correction, portability, and objection, which you can exercise by contacting us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to verification images is restricted to the account owner and authorized safety staff. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Pines is strictly for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we remove accounts found to belong to minors.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
